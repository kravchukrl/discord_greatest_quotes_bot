
Q - Why?
A - Initially, the author of the bot and their friendgroup wanted to commemorate iconic quotes from their groupchat, by pinning them in discord
And eventually ran into the limit of 50 pinned messages
To alter that, a tradition arose, that whenever a message would get a certain number of pin (📌) reactions, a dedicated member would add those messages to a dedicated channel
PINAS is the automation of that role

Q - Is it Open Source?
A - [Yup.](https://github.com/kravchukrl/discord_greatest_quotes_bot) 

Q - Why does it say that the channel is restricted?
A - For privacy reasons, and to avoid duplication, channels can be set as restricted, i.e. to disallow pinning of messages from those channels
