Hello, I'm PIN.AS, Pinning Importnant Notices Automated System

My goal in life is helping to save memorable quotes and messages of users in a server, while leaving the choice in the hands of the masses

ヽ(✿ﾟ▽ﾟ)ノ

Main functionality:
• Whenever the content of a message is deemed to be funny/compromising enough to warrant being remembered, users can react to the message using the pin (📌) emoji. 
• If the number of pin reactions reaches the pin threshold, the message is sent to the {quotes} channel. For {server} the threshold sands at {number}. 
• PINAS supports resending of gifs and images, crediting the author.

Avaliable commands:

/faq - inFrequently Asked Questions

/hello - Say hi to our beloved PINAS:3

/help - explaining PINAS functionality and commands

/speak - See if PINAS can offer any words of wisdom

/suggest -  Allows to suggest a quote, if it was spoken irl/in voice chat. After writing down the quote and the author, a vote will occur with a 6 hour limit. Whichever option reaches threshold number of votes, will decide whether the quote is added
